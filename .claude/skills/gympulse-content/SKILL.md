# Writing and designing GymPulse AI content

## Audience and voice
- Readers are Nigerian gym owners, managers and trainers, usually on a phone.
- Plain English, short sentences, concrete examples from gym life (check-ins, renewals, PT clients).
  No jargon ("churn", "SaaS", "webhook"), no hype, no emojis in pitches or formal emails.
- Lead with the problem in their words: *"Most members don't cancel. They just stop coming."*
- One clear next step: the 30-day free trial at usegympulseai.com, or a short demo.
- When a gym already uses GymMaster, say GymPulse works **alongside** it; it doesn't replace it.
- Messages to a named prospect are short, friendly and specific; write them in the founder's voice and keep their
  own words when they supply them.

## Honesty (non-negotiable)
- Only claim what works today. WhatsApp: say it works only once templates are approved; until then, email.
- No invented numbers. Demo figures must be labelled ("figures from the GymPulse AI demo gym").
- No customer logos, testimonials or partnerships that don't exist (no HMO partnerships).
- Case studies tell what really happened, with the source of the data stated (e.g. "from his trainer's records").
  Illustrative visuals are labelled or obviously illustrative.
- Comparisons with known brands ("like Paystack, but for keeping members") are spoken analogies only:
  never use another company's logo, design or wording, and never imply a partnership.

## Privacy and consent
- Members appear by first name only, and only with their permission; never health details in public.
- Don't name a gym publicly (e.g. Robo Fitness) without the owner's agreement; make a private version for them
  and a public version that says "a gym in Lagos".
- Never show the registered office street address, phone numbers or bank details in public content.

## Formats that work
- **LinkedIn:** story post + document (PDF) or image carousel; link in the first comment.
- **Instagram:** 1080×1080 carousel (hook, what happened, the pitch) + 1080×1920 story; link in bio.
- **WhatsApp Status / messages:** one image, two lines, one link.
- **Print:** A5 flyer, matte 250–300gsm, QR code to usegympulseai.com; check the QR on the first proof.
- **Video:** 4:5 for feeds, big readable text (phone-first), smooth fades, music, no fast speed-ups;
  render frames from HTML and check them before sending.

## Brand
Every piece follows the "modern, human-designed, never AI slop" checklist in `CLAUDE.md`.
The "Register" look: near-black `#0E0F12` background, warm paper-white `#F2EFE6` text, ruled lines and small corners,
lime `#B7F34A` as the single accent, IBM Plex Sans for words and IBM Plex Mono for numbers (fonts in
`.claude/skills/canvas-design/canvas-fonts`), logo files in the repository root and `static/`.
