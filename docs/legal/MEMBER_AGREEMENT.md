## 2. Fees, renewal and cancellation
2.1 Fees are paid in advance for each membership period, in Nigerian naira, by the methods the Gym accepts.
2.2 Your membership ends on the end date unless you renew it by paying for the next period. The Gym will remind you before it ends. Nothing is taken from your account automatically unless you separately agree to it in writing.
2.3 You may stop at any time by not renewing. Fees already paid for a period that has started are not refundable, except where the Gym agrees or the law requires (including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018).
2.4 If the Gym offers membership freezes (for travel, illness or injury), the Gym's freeze policy applies. Ask at the front desk.
2.5 The Gym may change its prices with reasonable notice. A change never affects a period you have already paid for.

## 3. Health declaration
3.1 You confirm that the answers in your health questions below are true, and that you will tell the Gym's staff or your trainer if your health changes.
3.2 If you answered "yes" to any health question, the Gym strongly advises you to get clearance from a doctor before you start or increase exercise. By signing, you confirm you have done so or that you choose to exercise on your own responsibility.
3.3 You will stop exercising and tell a member of staff straight away if you feel chest pain, severe breathlessness, dizziness, faintness or sudden pain.

## 4. Assumption of risk
4.1 Exercise carries risks, including muscle strains, sprains, fractures, heart problems and, rarely, serious injury or death. You understand these risks and choose to exercise.
4.2 You will use equipment only as intended, ask staff when unsure, warm up and cool down, and train within your ability.

## 5. Waiver and release
5.1 To the fullest extent permitted by Nigerian law, you release the Gym, its owners, staff and trainers from claims for injury, illness, loss or damage that result from the normal risks of exercise or from your own actions, including your failure to follow these terms, the Gym's rules or staff instructions.
5.2 This waiver does not exclude the Gym's liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, for gross negligence or wilful misconduct, or for anything the law does not allow to be excluded.
5.3 You are responsible for any damage you, or a guest you bring, deliberately or carelessly cause to the Gym's property or to other people.

## 6. Emergency care
6.1 You authorise the Gym's staff to give you first aid, and to call your emergency contact and emergency services, if you are unwell or injured at the Gym.
6.2 Any cost of emergency transport or medical treatment is yours (or your health insurer's or HMO's).

## 7. Personal belongings
The Gym is not responsible for the loss of, theft of or damage to personal belongings or vehicles on its premises, including in lockers, unless caused by the Gym's negligence. Do not leave valuables unattended.

## 8. Gym rules
You agree to follow the Gym's rules and the instructions of its staff, including:
- Check in each time you visit, using your own membership only.
- Wear proper training clothes and closed training shoes on the gym floor.
- Wipe equipment after use and return weights and equipment to their place.
- Treat members and staff with respect: no harassment, abuse, violence or discrimination.
- No smoking, alcohol, illegal drugs or weapons on the premises; do not train under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Do not film or photograph other members or staff without their permission, and never in changing rooms.
- Only the Gym's approved trainers may give personal training on the premises.
- Report any injury, hazard or broken equipment to staff straight away.

## 9. Your personal data
9.1 The Gym is responsible for your personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. It keeps your details (name, contact details, membership, visits, payments, this signed Agreement, your health answers and emergency contact) to manage your membership, keep you safe and stay in touch with you about your membership.
9.2 **Health answers.** Your health answers are sensitive personal data. By signing, you give your explicit consent for the Gym to keep them and use them only for your safety. They are shared only with staff and trainers who need them, and never used for marketing. You can withdraw this consent by asking the Gym to delete them, but the Gym may then ask for a doctor's clearance before you train.
9.3 The Gym uses GymPulse AI (GYMPULSE AI LTD, Nigeria) to store these details and send you membership messages by email or WhatsApp. GymPulse AI acts only on the Gym's instructions. Your details are stored on secure servers in the European Union (Germany), and some services that send messages are in the United States, under contracts that protect your data.
9.4 The Gym keeps your details while you are a member and for a reasonable time afterwards (signed agreements are kept as a record of what you agreed). You can ask the Gym to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop marketing messages, at any time. Reply STOP to a WhatsApp message, or use the unsubscribe link in an email, to stop those messages. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

## 10. Members under 18
10.1 A person under 18 may only become a member if their parent or guardian signs this Agreement for them. The parent or guardian agrees to it on the young member's behalf, consents to the Gym keeping the young member's details (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, section 31), and is responsible for their fees and conduct.
10.2 Members under 18 must follow any extra rules the Gym sets for them, such as training only with a trainer or at set times.
