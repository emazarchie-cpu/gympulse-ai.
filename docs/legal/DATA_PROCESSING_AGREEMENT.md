# Data Processing Agreement

This agreement forms part of the GymPulse AI Terms of Service and is made between:
- **the gym** that uses GymPulse AI (the data controller), and
- **GYMPULSE AI LTD**, a company incorporated in Nigeria (RC 9996401), registered office in Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria ("GymPulse AI", the data processor).

It covers the personal data of the gym's members, former members, prospects, staff and trainers that the gym puts into GymPulse AI ("member data"). It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (the "NDPA"), in particular section 29 (processing by a data processor), and the NDPC's directives under it.

## 1. What is processed
- **People:** the gym's members, former members, prospects (for example referred friends), staff and trainers, emergency contacts, and contacts at partner companies and HMOs.
- **Data:** name, phone, email, photo, age, weight, height, fitness goal, progress records, membership plan and dates, payments, check-in times, messages, exit reasons, loyalty points, signed membership agreements, and, where the member gives it, health answers and an emergency contact.
- **Sensitive data:** health answers are sensitive personal data. They are processed only to keep the member safe, on the basis of the member's explicit consent obtained by the gym (NDPA section 30).
- **Purpose:** to provide GymPulse AI to the gym: member records, attendance tracking, risk estimates, messages and emails, loyalty, classes, challenges, referrals, membership agreements, reports and related features.
- **Duration:** for as long as the gym uses GymPulse AI, plus the deletion period in clause 9.

## 2. The gym's responsibilities (controller)
The gym confirms that:
- it has a lawful basis under NDPA section 25 for each piece of member data it adds;
- it has told its members that it uses GymPulse AI and how (NDPA section 27);
- it has members' consent, or another lawful basis, to email and message them, and honours opt-outs;
- it collects health data only with the member's explicit consent and only for their safety;
- it does not add members under 18 without a parent's or guardian's consent (NDPA section 31);
- it gives GymPulse AI access only to staff who need it, and removes access when they leave;
- it is responsible for any registration with, filings to and fees payable to the NDPC that apply to it as a data controller.

## 3. GymPulse AI's responsibilities (processor)
GymPulse AI will:
1. process member data only on the gym's documented instructions (using the app counts as an instruction), and not for its own purposes;
2. make sure everyone with access is bound by confidentiality;
3. keep appropriate technical and organisational security in place (clause 5);
4. use sub-processors only under clause 4;
5. help the gym respond to members' rights requests (access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability);
6. help the gym with security, breach notification and data protection impact assessments;
7. delete or return member data at the end (clause 9);
8. give the gym the information needed to show compliance, and allow a reasonable audit once a year on 30 days' notice;
9. tell the gym straight away if it believes an instruction breaks the NDPA;
10. where records are needed as evidence, provide a certificate about the computer system that produced them, as required by section 84 of the Evidence Act 2011.

## 4. Sub-processors
4.1 The gym authorises these sub-processors for member data:

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in and file storage | Frankfurt, Germany (EU) |
| Render | Hosting the app | Ohio, United States |
| Resend | Sending emails | United States |
| Anthropic | The Archie assistant | United States |
| GitHub | Scheduled reminders and reports | United States |
| Sentry | Error reports (personal data removed) | United States |
| Meta (WhatsApp) | WhatsApp messages, only if the gym uses WhatsApp | United States and other Meta locations |

4.2 GymPulse AI will give at least 14 days' notice by email before adding or replacing a sub-processor, and the gym may object on reasonable data protection grounds. If the objection cannot be resolved, the gym may close its account and receive a refund of fees for the unused part of any period paid for.
4.3 GymPulse AI imposes data protection duties on each sub-processor that are no less protective than this agreement, and remains responsible to the gym for them.

## 5. Security measures
- HTTPS for all traffic; data encrypted at rest by the database provider.
- Each gym can only reach its own records (database row-level security); branch staff see only their branch.
- Passwords hashed by the login provider, never stored in plain text.
- Admin access restricted to named people, with every admin action logged.
- Secret keys kept only on servers, never in the app or the code repository.
- Error reports have personal data removed before they leave our systems.
- Signed agreements are stored with a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact text, so any change can be detected.
- Regular backups by the database provider, and periodic access reviews.

## 6. Personal data breaches
GymPulse AI will notify the gym without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting member data. The notice will say what happened, the data and people affected, the likely consequences and the steps taken. This lets the gym notify the NDPC within 72 hours and inform affected members where required (NDPA section 40).

## 7. Transfers outside Nigeria
Member data is stored in the European Union (Germany) and some is processed in the United States by the sub-processors in clause 4. GymPulse AI transfers member data outside Nigeria only where NDPA sections 41–43 allow it, relying on each sub-processor's data processing terms with appropriate safeguards (including standard contractual clauses), keeps a record of these transfers, and will give the gym a copy of the relevant safeguards on request.

## 8. Risk estimates
GymPulse AI's risk estimates support the gym's own decisions about whom to contact. They are not used to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on members without human involvement (NDPA section 37).

## 9. End of the agreement
When the gym closes its account, it can export its data for 30 days. GymPulse AI will then delete member data within 90 days, and from backups within a further 30 days, unless the law requires it to be kept, and will confirm the deletion in writing on request.

## 10. Liability
Each party is responsible for its own breaches of the NDPA. The limits in the Terms of Service apply, except where the law does not allow them.

## 11. Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, and disputes are resolved as set out in the Terms of Service.
