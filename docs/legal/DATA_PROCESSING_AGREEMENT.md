# Data Processing Agreement

This agreement forms part of the GymPulse AI Terms of Service between:
- **the gym** (the data controller), and
- **GymPulse AI**, GYMPULSE AI LTD (RC 9996401), Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria (the data processor).

It covers the personal data of the gym's members, staff and prospects that the gym puts into GymPulse AI ("member data"). It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (the "NDPA"), in particular section 29 (processors).

## 1. What is processed
- **People:** the gym's members, former members, prospects (for example referred friends), staff and trainers, and contacts at partner companies.
- **Data:** name, phone, email, photo, age, weight, height, fitness goal, progress records, membership plan and dates, payments, check-in times, messages, exit reasons, loyalty points.
- **Purpose:** to provide GymPulse AI to the gym: member records, attendance tracking, risk estimates, messages and emails, loyalty, challenges, referrals, reports and related features.
- **Duration:** for as long as the gym uses GymPulse AI, plus the deletion period in clause 9.

## 2. The gym's responsibilities (controller)
The gym confirms that:
- it has a lawful basis under NDPA section 25 for each piece of member data it adds;
- it has told its members that it uses GymPulse AI and how (NDPA section 27);
- it has members' consent, or another lawful basis, to email and message them, and honours opt-outs;
- it does not add sensitive data (for example health conditions) unless it has explicit consent and a real need;
- it does not add members under 18 without a parent's or guardian's consent;
- it only gives GymPulse AI access to staff who need it.

## 3. GymPulse AI's responsibilities (processor)
GymPulse AI will:
1. process member data only on the gym's documented instructions (using the app counts as an instruction), and not for its own purposes;
2. make sure everyone with access is bound by confidentiality;
3. keep appropriate security in place (clause 5);
4. only use sub-processors under clause 4;
5. help the gym respond to members' rights requests (access, correction, deletion, objection, portability);
6. help the gym with security, breach notification and data protection impact assessments;
7. delete or return member data at the end (clause 9);
8. give the gym the information needed to show compliance, and allow a reasonable audit once a year on 30 days' notice;
9. tell the gym straight away if it believes an instruction breaks the NDPA.

## 4. Sub-processors
The gym authorises the sub-processors listed in the Privacy Policy (Supabase, Render, Resend, Anthropic, GitHub, Sentry, and Meta/WhatsApp if connected). GymPulse AI will give 14 days' notice before adding or replacing one, and the gym may object on reasonable data protection grounds. GymPulse AI imposes the same data protection duties on each sub-processor and remains responsible for them.

## 5. Security measures
- HTTPS for all traffic; data encrypted at rest by the database provider.
- Each gym can only reach its own records (database row-level security).
- Passwords hashed by the login provider, never stored in plain text.
- Admin access restricted to named GymPulse AI staff, with every admin action logged.
- Secret keys kept only on servers, never in the app or the code repository.
- Error reports have personal data removed before they leave our systems.
- Regular backups once the production database plan allows, and access reviews.

## 6. Personal data breaches
GymPulse AI will notify the gym without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting member data. The notice will cover what happened, the data and people affected, likely consequences and the steps taken. This lets the gym notify the NDPC within 72 hours and inform affected members where required (NDPA section 40).

## 7. Transfers outside Nigeria
Some sub-processors process data outside Nigeria. GymPulse AI will only transfer member data where NDPA sections 41–43 are met, for example through contracts with appropriate safeguards, and will keep a record of these transfers.

## 8. Risk estimates
GymPulse AI's risk estimates support the gym's own decisions about whom to contact. They are not used to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on members without human involvement (NDPA section 37).

## 9. End of the agreement
When the gym closes its account, it can export its data for 30 days. GymPulse AI will then delete member data within 90 days, and from backups within a further 30 days, unless the law requires it to be kept, and will confirm deletion on request.

## 10. Liability
Each party is responsible for its own breaches of the NDPA. The limits in the Terms of Service apply, except where the law does not allow them.
