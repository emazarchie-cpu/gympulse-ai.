# Subscription Agreement

**THIS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT** ("Agreement") is made on the date it is signed electronically by the Subscriber

**BETWEEN**

**GYMPULSE AI LTD**, a private limited company incorporated in Nigeria under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 with registration number RC 9996401, whose office is in Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria ("the Company", "GymPulse AI", "we", "us"), of the one part;

## Background
**WHEREAS:**
A. The Company owns and operates GymPulse AI, online software that helps gyms manage and keep their members.
B. The Subscriber wishes to use GymPulse AI for its business, after a free trial, on a paid subscription.
C. The Parties wish to record the terms on which the Company provides, and the Subscriber pays for, GymPulse AI.

**NOW IT IS AGREED** as follows:

## 1. Definitions
In this Agreement:
- **"Service"** means GymPulse AI: its web application, member app and pages, automatic messages, reports, risk scores and related tools, as updated from time to time.
- **"Plan"** means the subscription plan chosen by the Subscriber, priced by number of Members.
- **"Members"** means the active members in the Subscriber's GymPulse AI account, plus members archived in the previous 30 days.
- **"Subscription Period"** means each month (or, if the Subscriber chooses to pay ahead, each year) paid for.
- **"Subscriber Data"** means data the Subscriber or its members put into the Service, including members' personal data.
- **"Fees"** means the price of the Plan shown at checkout when the Subscriber pays.
- The Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement published at usegympulseai.com ("the Policies") form part of this Agreement. If they conflict with this Agreement on plans, Fees or payment, this Agreement prevails.

## 2. Commencement and term
2.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date the Subscriber signs it, which is at the Subscriber's first payment after its free trial.
2.2 It is a **month-to-month subscription with no fixed term and no yearly commitment**. It continues for as long as the Subscriber keeps paying, and the Subscriber does not need to sign again for each payment.
2.3 New Subscribers receive a 30-day free trial (or a pilot period agreed in writing) without charge or obligation.

## 3. The Service
3.1 The Company grants the Subscriber a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Service for the Subscriber's own business during each paid Subscription Period.
3.2 Every Plan includes every feature. Plans differ only by the number of Members covered, message allowances and the level of support.
3.3 The Company shall provide the Service with reasonable skill and care, keep it secure, back up Subscriber Data, fix faults within a reasonable time, and aim to keep the Service available at all times, with planned maintenance outside busy hours where possible.
3.4 The Company shall provide support by email (hello@usegympulseai.com) and WhatsApp during Nigerian business hours, Monday to Saturday.
3.5 The Service is software. The Company does not operate the Subscriber's gym and does not guarantee any number of members, renewals or income.

## 4. Plans and member limits
4.1 The Subscriber shall choose a Plan that covers all its Members. A Plan covering fewer Members than the Subscriber has cannot be chosen.
4.2 If the Subscriber grows past its Plan, it has 30 days to move to the correct Plan, during which it may add up to 10% more Members. After that, new Members cannot be added until it upgrades; everything else keeps working.
4.3 The Subscriber shall not split one business into several accounts, share an account between separate businesses, or otherwise arrange to pay for fewer Members than it has. If this happens, the Company may move the Subscriber to the correct Plan and charge the difference from when it began.

## 5. Fees and payment
5.1 The Subscriber shall pay the Fees in Nigerian naira, in advance for each Subscription Period, by bank transfer or by card or USSD through Paystack. Fees include any value added tax that applies.
5.2 **The Service does not renew or charge automatically.** The Company reminds the Subscriber before each Subscription Period ends; the Subscriber renews by paying for the next one.
5.3 If the Subscriber does not renew, access to the Service is paused at the end of the paid Subscription Period (including staff and trainer access, the member app and public pages, automatic messages and reports). Subscriber Data remains safe and access returns on payment.
5.4 The Company shall give at least 30 days' notice of any change in Fees. No change affects a Subscription Period already paid for.

## 6. Right to cancel and refunds
6.1 **The Subscriber has the right to cancel at any time**, by not renewing or by telling the Company by email. There is no cancellation fee, notice period or minimum term beyond the Subscription Period already paid for.
6.2 Fees for a Subscription Period that has started are not refundable, except that: (a) a payment made twice or by mistake is refunded in full; (b) if the Service is unavailable for more than 7 consecutive days through the Company's fault, the Company shall, at the Subscriber's choice, extend the Plan by the days lost or refund that part of the Fees; (c) a year paid in advance and cancelled within 14 days because the Service does not work as described is refunded for the unused months; and (d) any refund required by law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 where it applies, is made.

## 7. Subscriber's obligations
The Subscriber shall:
(a) give correct business and billing details and keep its logins confidential, being responsible for everyone it gives access to;
(b) have a lawful basis under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 for every Member it adds, and their consent (or another lawful basis) for the messages it sends them through the Service, and tell its members that it uses GymPulse AI;
(c) use the Service lawfully, and not to send unsolicited messages, harass anyone, or breach the rules of WhatsApp, email providers or health maintenance organisations (HMOs);
(d) check claims, reports and messages before relying on them or sending them to members, HMOs or companies.

## 8. Data protection and Subscriber Data
8.1 The Subscriber owns Subscriber Data. In respect of members' personal data, the Subscriber is the data controller and the Company is its data processor, and the Company processes it only to provide the Service and on the Subscriber's instructions, as set out in the Data Processing Agreement and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
8.2 The Subscriber may export its members, visits and payments at any time. When an account is closed, the Subscriber may export Subscriber Data for at least 30 days; it is then deleted within 90 days of closure as described in the Privacy Policy, unless the law requires otherwise.
8.3 The Company may use anonymised, aggregated statistics that identify no gym or person to improve the Service.

## 9. Intellectual property
The Company owns GymPulse AI, including its software, designs, models and brand. Nothing in this Agreement transfers any of it to the Subscriber.

## 10. Estimates, not guarantees
Risk scores, predictions, suggested actions and money figures marked "estimate" are guides to help the Subscriber decide whom to contact. They are not promises of results, and decisions about members remain the Subscriber's.

## 11. Limitation of liability and indemnity
11.1 Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, loss of members or loss of goodwill.
11.2 Each Party's total liability under this Agreement in any 12 months is limited to the Fees paid in those 12 months.
11.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for the Subscriber's obligation to pay Fees, for breach of clause 7(b), or for anything that cannot be limited under Nigerian law.
11.4 The Subscriber shall indemnify the Company against reasonable losses arising from a claim by a member, regulator or third party caused by the Subscriber adding or messaging Members without a lawful basis.

## 12. Confidentiality
Each Party shall keep the other's non-public business information confidential and use it only for this Agreement. This obligation continues for 2 years after the Agreement ends.

## 13. Termination
13.1 The Subscriber may end this Agreement at any time under clause 6.1.
13.2 The Company may end it by giving 30 days' notice by email, and shall refund Fees for any part of a Subscription Period not used.
13.3 Either Party may end it immediately by notice if the other commits a material breach and does not remedy it within 14 days of being asked to, or becomes insolvent.
13.4 Clauses 8, 9, 11, 12 and 15 survive termination.

## 14. Force majeure
Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including power or network failure, failure of payment or messaging providers, strikes, natural disasters, epidemics or government action. The affected Party shall notify the other and take reasonable steps to limit the effect.

## 15. Governing law and dispute resolution
15.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
15.2 The Parties shall first try to settle any dispute by good-faith negotiation for 30 days. If that fails, either Party may refer it to mediation at the Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse. If mediation does not settle it within 60 days, the courts of Lagos State shall have jurisdiction.

16.2 The Company may update this Agreement for future Subscription Periods on at least 30 days' notice; changes apply from the Subscriber's next renewal and the Subscriber may choose not to renew.
16.3 Notices are given by email: to the Subscriber at its billing email, and to the Company at hello@usegympulseai.com.
16.4 The Subscriber may not assign this Agreement without the Company's written consent. The Company may assign it to a successor that takes over the Service, and shall notify the Subscriber.
