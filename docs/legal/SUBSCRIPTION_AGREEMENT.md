# Subscription Agreement

This Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is between **GYMPULSE AI LTD** (RC 9996401), a company registered in Nigeria with its office in Calabar, Cross River State ("GymPulse AI", "we", "us"), and the gym business named at signing ("the Gym", "you"). It applies when you subscribe to a paid GymPulse AI plan. It sits alongside our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement, which form part of it. If they conflict, this Agreement wins on fees, plans and payment.

## 1. What you are buying
1.1 GymPulse AI is online software that helps gyms keep their members. It records members, plans, payments and check-ins, estimates which members may stop attending, suggests and sends follow-ups, and provides reports, a member app and related tools ("the Service").
1.2 Every plan includes every feature. Plans differ only by the number of members they cover, the monthly message allowances and the level of support, as shown on the plan at the time you pay.
1.3 The Service is software. We do not run your gym, contact members on our own initiative or guarantee any number of members, renewals or income.

## 2. Free trial and pilot
2.1 New gyms get a 30-day free trial (or a pilot period we agree in writing). Nothing is charged during it and you are not obliged to subscribe.
2.2 If you do not subscribe when the trial ends, access is paused. Your data is kept for at least 30 days so you can subscribe or export it, as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 3. Plan, member limit and fair use
3.1 Your plan is chosen by your number of members. "Members" means active members in your GymPulse AI account, plus members archived in the last 30 days.
3.2 You may not choose a plan that covers fewer members than you have. If you grow past your plan's limit, you have 30 days to move to the right plan; during those days you may add up to 10% more members. After that, new members cannot be added until you upgrade. Everything else keeps working.
3.3 You will not split one gym into several accounts, share one account between separate businesses, or use other means to pay for fewer members than you have. If we find this, we may move you to the correct plan and charge the difference from when it started.

## 4. Fees and payment
4.1 The fee is the price of your plan and billing period (monthly or yearly) shown at checkout, in Nigerian naira. Prices include any value added tax that applies.
4.2 Fees are paid in advance, by bank transfer or by card/USSD through Paystack. Your plan starts when payment is confirmed. A plan paid during the free trial starts when the trial ends.
4.3 Plans do not renew or charge you automatically. Before your paid period ends we remind you by email and in the app; you renew by paying for the next period.
4.4 If you do not renew, access to the Service is paused when your paid period ends for you, your staff and trainers, your members' app and public pages, and automatic messages and reports. Your data stays safe and everything returns as soon as you renew.
4.5 We will give you at least 30 days' notice of any price change. A change never affects a period you have already paid for.

## 5. Refunds and cancellation
5.1 You can stop at any time by not renewing. There is no cancellation fee and no minimum term beyond the period you paid for.
5.2 Fees for a period that has started are not refundable, except: (a) a payment made twice or by mistake is refunded in full; (b) if the Service is unavailable for more than 7 days in a row through our fault, we extend your plan by the lost days or, if you prefer, refund that part of the fee; (c) where Nigerian law requires a refund.
5.3 A yearly plan cancelled in its first 14 days because the Service does not work as described is refunded for the unused months.

## 6. What we do
6.1 Provide the Service with reasonable skill and care, keep it secure, and fix faults within a reasonable time.
6.2 Aim to keep the Service available at all times. Planned maintenance is done outside your busiest hours where possible.
6.3 Provide support by email (hello@usegympulseai.com) and WhatsApp during Nigerian business hours, Monday to Saturday, at the level of your plan.
6.4 Keep your data confidential, process your members' data only to provide the Service and on your instructions (Data Processing Agreement), and back it up.
6.5 Tell you in advance about changes that reduce what you have paid for.

## 7. What you do
7.1 Give correct business and billing details, and keep your logins private. You are responsible for everyone you give access to.
7.2 Have a lawful basis under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 for every member you add, and their permission (or another lawful basis) for the messages you send them through GymPulse AI. Tell your members you use GymPulse AI.
7.3 Use the Service lawfully, and not to send spam, harass anyone or break the rules of WhatsApp, email providers or health insurers (HMOs).
7.4 Check that claims, reports and messages are correct before you rely on them or send them to members, HMOs or companies.

## 8. Your data
8.1 You own your data and your members' data. We own GymPulse AI, its software, designs and models.
8.2 You can export your members, visits and payments at any time. When your account is closed, you can export your data for at least 30 days; it is then deleted within 90 days of closure as described in the Privacy Policy, unless the law requires us to keep it.
8.3 We may use anonymised, combined statistics that identify no gym or person to improve the Service.

## 9. Estimates, not guarantees
Risk scores, predictions, suggested actions and money figures marked "estimate" are guides to help you decide whom to contact. They are not promises of results. Decisions about your members remain yours.
