# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GymPulse AI ("we", "us") handles personal data. It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (the "NDPA") and the rules of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

**Who we are.** GymPulse AI, [registered company name, RC number, registered address]. Contact for privacy matters: privacy@usegympulseai.com. Our Data Protection Officer is [name], dpo@usegympulseai.com.

## 1. Two kinds of data, two roles

| Whose data | Examples | Our role |
|---|---|---|
| **Gym owners and their staff** (our customers) | Name, business name, email, phone, login details, settings | We are the **data controller** |
| **Gym members** (the gym's customers) | Name, phone, email, age, weight, height, fitness goal, photo, plan and payment records, check-in times, messages | The **gym is the data controller**. We are the gym's **data processor** and act only on the gym's instructions (see the Data Processing Agreement) |

If you are a gym member, the gym you train at decides how your data is used. Contact them first. We will help them respond.

## 2. What we collect about gym owners and staff
- Account details: name, business name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider).
- Business settings: logo, brand colours, contact details, bank details and payment links you choose to add.
- Usage data: pages used, log-in times, and technical error reports (without member details).

## 3. Why we use it (lawful basis, NDPA section 25)
- **To provide the service you signed up for** (contract): running your account, sending account emails, monthly reports and service notices.
- **Legitimate interests:** keeping the service secure, fixing errors, and improving features. We do not use your data in ways you would not reasonably expect.
- **Consent:** for optional marketing from us. You can opt out at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** where the law requires us to keep or share information.

## 4. How member data is used
Only to provide GymPulse AI to the gym: showing members, tracking check-ins, estimating who may stop attending, drafting and sending the gym's messages and emails, loyalty points, reports and similar features. We **never sell** member data, never use it for our own marketing, and never use it to contact members on our own behalf.

**Risk estimates.** GymPulse AI estimates which members may stop attending. These are estimates to help the gym decide whom to contact. They are not automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on members (NDPA section 37), and a person at the gym always decides what to do.

We use trusted providers to run the service. Each one only receives what it needs.

## 6. Data sent outside Nigeria (NDPA sections 41–43)
Some providers store or process data outside Nigeria. We only use providers that give appropriate protection, through contracts with data protection clauses, and we keep a record of these transfers.

## 7. How long we keep data
- Gym accounts: while the account is open, then deleted within 90 days of closure (backups within a further 30 days), unless the law requires longer.
- Member data: for as long as the gym keeps it in GymPulse AI. When a gym deletes a member or closes its account, the data is deleted as above.
- Email and message logs: up to 24 months.
- Error reports: up to 90 days.

## 8. Security (NDPA section 39)
Encrypted connections (HTTPS) and encryption at rest by our database provider; each gym can only access its own records (row-level security); passwords hashed by the login provider; admin access limited to named staff and logged; secret keys kept only on our servers.

## 9. Data breaches (NDPA section 40)
If a breach is likely to put people's rights at risk, we will tell the affected gym without undue delay and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware, so the gym can meet its 72-hour duty to notify the NDPC. We will notify the NDPC directly where we are the controller.

## 10. Your rights (NDPA sections 34–38)
You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, move it to another provider, or withdraw consent. Email privacy@usegympulseai.com. We reply within 30 days. Members should contact their gym, and we will help the gym respond. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

## 11. Emails to members
Every email a gym sends through GymPulse AI includes an unsubscribe link. Members who unsubscribe are never emailed again through GymPulse AI.

## 12. Children
GymPulse AI is for businesses. Gyms must not add members under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian (NDPA section 31).

## 13. Changes
We will tell gym owners about important changes by email and in the app before they take effect.
