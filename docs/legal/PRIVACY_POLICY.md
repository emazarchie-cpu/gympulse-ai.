# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GymPulse AI handles personal data. It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (the "NDPA") and the regulations and directives of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

**Who we are.** GYMPULSE AI LTD, a company incorporated in Nigeria (RC 9996401), registered office in Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria ("GymPulse AI", "we", "us"). Privacy contact: hello@usegympulseai.com.

**Data Protection Officer.** Emmanuel Archibong, Director · emmanuel@usegympulseai.com.

## 1. Two kinds of data, two roles

| Whose data | Examples | Our role |
|---|---|---|
| **Gym owners and their staff** (our customers) | Name, business name, email, phone, login details, settings, billing details | We are the **data controller** |
| **Gym members** (the gym's customers) | Name, phone, email, age, weight, height, fitness goal, photo, plan and payment records, check-in times, messages, signed membership agreements, health answers and emergency contact | The **gym is the data controller**. We are the gym's **data processor** and act only on the gym's instructions (see the Data Processing Agreement) |

If you are a gym member, the gym you train at decides how your data is used. Contact the gym first. We will help it respond.

## 2. What we collect about gym owners and staff
- Account details: name, business name, email address, phone, and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider).
- Business settings: logo, brand colours, contact details, house rules, bank details and payment links you choose to add.
- Billing details: the plan, invoices and payments. Card details are handled by Paystack and never reach us.
- Signed agreements: the name, role, signature drawing, date, time, IP address and browser of the person who signs the Subscription Agreement.
- Usage data: pages used, sign-in times and technical error reports (without member details).

## 3. Why we use it (lawful basis, NDPA section 25)
- **Contract:** to provide the service you signed up for: running your account, billing, account emails, monthly reports and service notices.
- **Legitimate interests:** keeping the service secure, preventing misuse, fixing errors and improving features. We do not use your data in ways you would not reasonably expect.
- **Consent:** for optional marketing from us. You can opt out at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** where the law requires us to keep or share information, for example tax and accounting records.

## 4. How member data is used
Only to provide GymPulse AI to the gym: showing members, tracking check-ins, estimating who may stop attending, drafting and sending the gym's messages and emails, loyalty points, classes, signed membership agreements, reports and similar features. We **never sell** member data, never use it for our own marketing, and never contact members on our own behalf.

**Health answers** that members give when they sign a gym's Membership Agreement are sensitive personal data. They are collected by the gym with the member's explicit consent (NDPA section 30), used only for the member's safety, and visible only to the gym's staff who need them.

**Risk estimates.** GymPulse AI estimates which members may stop attending. These are estimates that help the gym decide whom to contact. They are not automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on members (NDPA section 37), and a person at the gym always decides what to do.

## 5. Who we share data with (service providers)
We use trusted providers to run the service. Each one only receives what it needs, under a written agreement that requires it to protect the data.

| Provider | What for | Where the data is processed |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in and file storage (all gym and member records) | Frankfurt, Germany (European Union) |
| Render | Hosting the app | Ohio, United States |
| Resend | Sending emails | United States |
| Anthropic | The Archie assistant (questions you ask about your gym) | United States |
| GitHub | Running scheduled reminders and reports | United States |
| Sentry | Error reports, with personal data removed | United States |
| Paystack | Taking payments from gyms for GymPulse AI plans | Nigeria |
| Meta (WhatsApp) | Sending WhatsApp messages, only if the gym uses WhatsApp | United States and other Meta locations |

We will tell gyms at least 14 days before adding or replacing a provider that handles member data.

## 6. Data sent outside Nigeria (NDPA sections 41–43)
Member and account data is stored in the European Union (Germany) and processed in the United States by the providers above. We only transfer personal data outside Nigeria where the NDPA allows it: each provider is bound by data processing terms with appropriate safeguards (including standard contractual clauses), and the European Union's data protection law applies to data stored in Germany. We keep a record of these transfers and their safeguards, and will give a copy of the relevant safeguards to the NDPC or to a gym on request.

## 7. How long we keep data
- **Gym accounts:** while the account is open; then deleted within 90 days of closure (and from backups within a further 30 days), unless the law requires longer.
- **Member data:** for as long as the gym keeps it in GymPulse AI. When a gym deletes a member or closes its account, the data is deleted as above.
- **Signed agreements and invoices:** kept for 6 years after the account closes, because they may be needed as evidence or for tax records.
- **Email and message logs:** up to 24 months.
- **Error reports:** up to 90 days.

## 8. Security (NDPA section 39)
Encrypted connections (HTTPS) and encryption at rest by our database provider; each gym can only reach its own records (row-level security); passwords hashed by the login provider; admin access limited to named people and logged; secret keys kept only on our servers; personal data removed from error reports.

## 9. Data breaches (NDPA section 40)
If a breach is likely to put people's rights at risk, we will tell the affected gym without undue delay and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware, so that the gym can meet its duty to notify the NDPC within 72 hours. Where we are the controller, we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours and tell affected people where required.

## 10. Your rights (NDPA sections 34–38)
You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format, or withdraw consent. Email our Data Protection Officer at emmanuel@usegympulseai.com or hello@usegympulseai.com. We reply within 30 days. Members should contact their gym, and we will help the gym respond. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

## 11. Messages to members
Every email a gym sends through GymPulse AI includes an unsubscribe link, and replying STOP to a WhatsApp message stops WhatsApp messages. Members who opt out are not messaged again through GymPulse AI on that channel.

## 12. Cookies
We use only essential cookies: one keeps you signed in for up to 30 days (it is deleted when you log out). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 13. Children
GymPulse AI is for businesses. Gyms must not add members under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian (NDPA section 31). A parent or guardian signs the Membership Agreement for a member under 18.

## 14. Changes
We will tell gym owners about important changes by email and in the app before they take effect, and show the version and date at the top of this policy.
