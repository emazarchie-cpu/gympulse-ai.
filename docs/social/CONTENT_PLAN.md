# GymPulse AI social content plan

Used by the founder and by Claude's daily post reminder. Follow `CLAUDE.md` (honesty, privacy, never
"AI slop") and `.claude/skills/gympulse-content`.

## Who we're talking to
Nigerian gym owners, managers and personal trainers, mostly in Lagos and Calabar, scrolling on a phone.
Secondary: fitness people and founders who share and comment.

## Five content pillars
1. **Retention lessons:** practical tips gym owners can use today (why members drift, when to call, what to say).
2. **Real stories:** what happened with real members and gyms, with permission (e.g. Noah, 33 days away, came back).
3. **Founder diary:** building GymPulse from Calabar and Lagos, gym visits, what owners said, honest wins and misses.
4. **Product in action:** one feature at a time on the demo gym, labelled as the demo gym.
5. **Conversation starters:** questions and polls that get owners talking.

## Engagement playbook
- **Hook in the first line.** A surprising truth or a specific moment, never a greeting.
- **End with one question** that's easy to answer from experience ("register or gut feel?").
- **Reply to every comment within the first hour,** and end replies with a question.
- **Polls on Friday** (LinkedIn and Instagram Stories); post the result on Monday.
- **Tag gyms and trainers only with their permission;** thank people who share.
- **Comment on 5 gym owners' posts a day** before posting your own: it lifts your reach.
- **Link in the first comment** on LinkedIn; link in bio on Instagram.
- **Reuse:** one idea → LinkedIn post → Instagram carousel → X line → WhatsApp Status.
- **Track weekly:** views, comments, profile visits, DMs from gym owners, demo bookings, trial sign-ups.

## Never
- Invented numbers, fake testimonials, partnerships that don't exist, claims that WhatsApp is live before it is.
- Member surnames, health details or faces without permission; a gym's name without the owner's OK.
- Another brand's logo or wording; engagement bait ("comment YES"); emojis as decoration; stock phrases.
