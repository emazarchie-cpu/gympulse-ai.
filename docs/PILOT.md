# Phase 5: Pilot and commercial readiness

Goal: 3 to 5 pilot gyms use GymPulse AI every week and can show a measurable operational or
financial change.

## What's in the app

- **Getting-started checklist** on the dashboard. It walks a new gym through seven steps:
  members, plans and fees, check-ins this week, trainers, first play, first outcome, and
  team and automation. It disappears once all seven are done, and can be hidden.
- **Retention operations** in Business Insights, covering the last 30 days:
  - coverage: the share of at-risk members with a play
  - median time to first contact
  - completion: plays finished with an outcome
  - conversion: kept ÷ (kept + cancelled)
- **Pilot scorecard** in Business Insights. Press "Save today as my pilot baseline" on day
  one, and GymPulse compares these numbers against it:
  - members
  - average visits
  - high-risk members
  - share at risk
  - renewal rate
  - coverage
  - conversion

  It says plainly that the comparison shows direction, not cause.
- **Export** in Business Insights: members and risk, plays, and payments as CSV, for pilot
  analysis.

## Running a pilot (for each gym)

**Week 0: set-up (about one hour with the owner)**
1. Create the account, then add members with plan, fee and end date, or import them.
2. Import past visits (Check-in → Import history) so the Risk Radar starts with real history.
3. Add trainers and staff, and leave automation off for the first week.
4. Press **Save today as my pilot baseline**.

**Weeks 1 to 2: build the habit**
- Front desk checks every member in.
- Owner or manager opens the dashboard daily and starts plays for the top priorities.
- Every play ends with Mark contacted, then Record outcome.
- Every renewal is recorded on Payments & Renewals.

**Weeks 3 to 6: automation and measurement**
- Turn on the renewal and high-risk rules, assigned to named staff.
- Review the weekly report together each week.
- Note what stops staff using it, and fix it.

**Week 6: review**
- Compare the pilot scorecard with the baseline.
- Collect revenue credited to plays and recovered revenue from Business Insights.
- Ask: would you pay for this, how much, and which part would you miss most?

## Measuring value honestly

- Credited revenue shows a play was involved in a renewal, not that the member would
  otherwise have left. For a fair comparison, look at renewal rate and attendance against
  the baseline. Where possible, also compare against the same months last year.
- Do not quote a prediction accuracy figure. The risk model is experimental and has not
  been tested on independent gym data.

## Packaging (to test in pilots, not final)

| Package | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Small independent gyms | Members, check-in, Risk Radar, dashboard, Archie |
| Growth | Established gyms | Everything in Starter, plus plays and follow-ups, payments and revenue outcomes, automation, team workload, weekly report |
| Multi-location | Larger operators | Everything in Growth, plus several locations, combined reporting and advanced controls (to build when needed) |

Set prices after pilots, based on members per gym, value shown and what owners say they'd pay.
